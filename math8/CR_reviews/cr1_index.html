What This Review Includes

Unit 1

  • Adding, subtracting, multiplying, and dividing integers
  • Using integers in real-life contexts
  • Showing clear steps and checking for reasonableness

Unit 2

  • Simplifying and comparing ratios
  • Solving rate and unit rate problems
  • Using proportional reasoning in real-life situations

Unit 3

  • Adding and subtracting fractions with unlike denominators
  • Multiplying and dividing fractions
  • Working with mixed numbers and order of operations

Skills to Focus On

  • Using efficient strategies
  • Showing all key steps in your work
  • Solving multi-step problems carefully
  • Using math vocabulary correctly
  • Checking whether your final answer makes sense

Success in This Review Looks Like…

  • I can solve problems from Units 1–3 using strategies that make sense to me.
  • I can show my thinking clearly and organize my work neatly.
  • I can apply what I know to multi-step questions.
  • I can identify what I already understand and what I still need to practise.

Student Reminder

This review is not just about getting answers. It is about noticing patterns, revisiting strategies, and building confidence across multiple units. Take your time, show your thinking, and use mistakes as information about what to practise next.
