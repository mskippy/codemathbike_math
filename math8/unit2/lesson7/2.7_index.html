Overview

This page is for reviewing everything we’ve learned in Unit 2: ratios, equivalent ratios, ratio word problems, rates, unit rates, and best buys. Your goal is to practice the skills, check your answers, and build a strong study sheet.

Learning Targets

  • Write ratios accurately (part-to-part and part-to-whole)
  • Simplify ratios and recognize equivalent ratios
  • Solve ratio word problems (including 3-term and “difference” problems)
  • Calculate rates and unit rates (with correct units)
  • Use unit pricing to identify the best buy and justify your choice

Key Vocabulary

  • Ratio — compares two or more amounts
  • Part-to-part — compares one part to another part
  • Part-to-whole — compares one part to the total
  • Equivalent ratios — same relationship, scaled up/down by the same factor
  • Rate — compares two different units (km per hour, dollars per item)
  • Unit rate / unit price — a rate “per 1” (or per 100 g, per litre, etc.)
  • Best buy — the option with the better unit rate (usually the lower cost per unit)

Big Idea

Ratios and unit rates help us compare fairly — even when numbers look confusing at first. The best choice is the one you can justify using math.

Write your units every time. Most mistakes happen when units are missing or mismatched.

How to Succeed on the Unit Test

  1. Start by identifying the type: ratio language, equivalent ratios, word problem, rate, or best buy
  2. Write the relationship clearly (what is compared to what?)
  3. Simplify when appropriate (but don’t change the meaning)
  4. Use “find 1 part” for many word problems
  5. For best buys: make units match → find unit price → justify your choice

If your answer has no units, it’s probably incomplete.

Study Sheet (Poster) Checklist

Your study sheet should include:

  • Definitions: ratio, equivalent ratio, rate, unit rate, unit price
  • Steps: simplify a ratio, check equivalence, solve a ratio word problem, find a unit rate
  • Examples: at least 1 worked example for each skill
  • Common mistakes: at least 2 mistakes to avoid (units, wrong comparison order, etc.)
