Overview

In this lesson, we learn how to calculate and use unit rates. A unit rate tells us how much happens for one unit, and helps us make predictions about distance, time, wages, work rate, and density.

Learning Targets

  • Understand rates as comparisons of different units
  • Calculate a unit rate (value per 1)
  • Use a unit rate to find other values
  • Apply unit rates to speed, wages, work rate, and density

Key Vocabulary

  • Rate — A comparison of two different units
  • Unit rate — A rate with a denominator of 1
  • Constant of proportionality — The fixed relationship between quantities
  • Density — Mass per unit of volume

Big Idea

A unit rate tells us how much happens for 1 unit. Once we know the unit rate, we can predict other values.

Find it. Circle it. Use it.

Lesson Flow

Warm-Up

  • Quick rate review (km per hour, dollars per hour, litres per minute)
  • What does “per” actually mean?

Class Examples

  • Speed (distance per time)
  • Work rate (units per hour)
  • Wage rate (dollars per hour)
  • Density (grams per cubic centimetre)

Guided Practice

  • Green problems — calculate and use unit rates
  • Blue problems — multi-step reasoning
  • Black problem — conversion + proportional reasoning

Wrap-Up

  • Why is the “per 1” value powerful?

How to Solve Unit Rate Problems

  1. Identify the two units (km & hours, dollars & hours, etc.)
  2. Divide to find the value per 1
  3. Write the unit rate clearly (include units!)
  4. Use the unit rate to answer the question

Most mistakes happen when students skip writing the units.
