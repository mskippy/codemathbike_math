Unit Overview

In this unit, we explore linear relationships by looking at patterns, graphs, and equations. We learn how to describe how things change, represent relationships in multiple ways, and use equations to make predictions.

What You’ll Learn

Core Math Skills

  • Recognize and extend linear patterns
  • Represent relationships using tables, graphs, and equations
  • Understand independent and dependent variables
  • Identify and interpret constant rate of change
  • Write equations in the form y = mx + b
  • Solve simple linear equations

Real-Life Contexts

  • Cost over time (subscriptions, rentals)
  • Distance, speed, and travel
  • Earnings and saving money
  • Comparing plans and options

BC Math 8 Alignment

Big Ideas

  • Relationships can be represented in multiple ways.
  • Generalizations describe patterns and relationships.
  • Mathematical models help us make predictions.

Curricular Content

  • Linear relations (tables, graphs, equations)
  • Coordinate plane and graphing
  • Rate of change
  • Solving linear equations

Curricular Competencies

  • Reasoning and analyzing patterns
  • Understanding and solving problems
  • Communicating mathematical thinking
  • Connecting math to real-world situations
