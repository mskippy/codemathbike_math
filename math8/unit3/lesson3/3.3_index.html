Overview

In this lesson, we focus on multiplying positive fractions and mixed numbers.

We will build procedural confidence (clear steps), practice simplifying efficiently, and extend to multiplying mixed numbers.

Learning Targets

  • Multiply fractions correctly
  • Simplify products fully
  • Cross-simplify before multiplying when helpful
  • Multiply fractions with whole numbers
  • Multiply mixed numbers (convert to improper fractions)

Warm Up

You will start with a procedural-thinking warm up (no actual numbers) to review adding and subtracting fractions before switching to multiplication.

Tip: When adding/subtracting, you need a common denominator. When multiplying, you do not.

Big Idea

Multiplying fractions means finding a part of a part.

Multiply across (top × top, bottom × bottom), then simplify. When possible, simplify first to make the math easier.

Lesson Flow

1. Multiplying Fractions

  • Multiply numerators
  • Multiply denominators
  • Simplify

2. Cross-Simplifying (Smart Simplify First)

  • Look for common factors diagonally
  • Reduce before you multiply
  • Then multiply across

3. Whole Numbers

  • Rewrite a whole number as a fraction over 1
  • Then multiply like normal

4. Mixed Numbers

  • Convert mixed numbers to improper fractions
  • Cross-simplify if possible
  • Multiply and simplify
  • Convert back to a mixed number if needed
