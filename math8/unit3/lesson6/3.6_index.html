Overview

Today we review everything we’ve learned about fraction operations.

You will use the Green • Blue • Black review handout to practice the skills and create a Fraction Operations Study Sheet to help you prepare for the Quest next class.

Learning Targets

  • Add and subtract fractions with unlike denominators
  • Multiply fractions and mixed numbers
  • Divide fractions using Keep • Change • Flip
  • Evaluate expressions using the order of operations
  • Simplify answers correctly

Green • Blue • Black Review

🟢 Green — Core Skills

  • Adding and subtracting fractions
  • Finding common denominators
  • Simplifying answers

🔵 Blue — Mixed Numbers & Strategy

  • Operations with mixed numbers
  • Converting between mixed and improper fractions
  • Cross simplifying when multiplying

⚫ Black — Multi-Step Expressions

  • Expressions with several fraction operations
  • Applying the order of operations
  • Combining multiple fraction skills in one problem

Start with Green. Move to Blue once you feel confident. Attempt Black when you are ready for more challenging problems.

Create Your Fraction Operations Study Sheet

Use your review questions and class notes to create a one-page study sheet.

Your study sheet should have two sides:

Side 1 — Fraction Operations

  • Adding fractions (steps + example)
  • Subtracting fractions (steps + example)
  • Multiplying fractions (steps + example)
  • Dividing fractions (Keep • Change • Flip)

Side 2 — Expressions

  • How to evaluate expressions with fractions
  • The order of operations
  • How to organize multi-step work clearly

Important Details to Include

Your study sheet should also remind you about:

  • Writing equivalent fractions
  • Finding common denominators
  • Cross simplifying before multiplying
  • Changing between mixed numbers and improper fractions
  • Always simplifying your final answer

The goal is to create a study sheet that helps you remember the process when solving fraction problems.
