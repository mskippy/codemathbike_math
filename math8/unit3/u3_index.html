Unit Overview

In this unit, we explore rational numbers — numbers that can be written as fractions, decimals, and percents. We will learn how to convert between forms, compare values, and apply these skills to real-life situations like money, discounts, and percent change.

What You’ll Learn

Core Math Skills

  • Recognize equivalent fractions, decimals, and percents
  • Convert fluently between forms
  • Compare and order rational numbers
  • Calculate percent of a number
  • Solve problems involving percent increase and decrease

Real-Life Contexts

  • Sales and discounts
  • Taxes and tips
  • Bank balances and changes
  • Growth and loss situations

BC Math 8 Alignment

Big Ideas

  • Number sense supports estimation and decision-making.
  • Proportional reasoning helps us understand percent relationships.
  • Mathematics helps us interpret and analyze real-world change.

Curricular Content

  • Fractions, decimals, and percents
  • Equivalent representations
  • Percent of a number
  • Percent increase and decrease

Curricular Competencies

  • Reasoning and analyzing
  • Communicating mathematical thinking
  • Connecting math to real life
  • Using efficient strategies
