Overview

In this lesson we combine our fraction skills with the order of operations.

You will evaluate expressions that include fractions, mixed numbers, and multiple operations. Success in this lesson comes from working carefully and rewriting the expression after each step.

Learning Targets

  • Apply the order of operations (BEDMAS)
  • Convert mixed numbers to improper fractions
  • Evaluate expressions with fractions
  • Simplify answers fully

Big Idea

When evaluating expressions with fractions we follow the same structure as whole numbers:

BEDMAS
Brackets
Exponents
Division & Multiplication (left → right)
Addition & Subtraction (left → right)

To stay organized, rewrite the entire expression after each step.

Strategy for Fraction Expressions

  1. Convert mixed numbers to improper fractions.
  2. Simplify inside brackets first.
  3. Multiply and divide from left to right.
  4. Add and subtract from left to right.
  5. Simplify whenever possible.

Green • Blue • Black Practice

🟢 Green — Core Structure

  • One set of brackets or simple operations
  • Apply BEDMAS correctly
  • Keep fractions (no decimals)

Complete all Green questions.

🔵 Blue — Mixed Numbers & Multi-Step Expressions

  • Convert mixed numbers to improper fractions
  • Evaluate brackets first
  • Use common denominators when needed

Complete all Blue questions.

⚫ Black — Strategic Expressions

  • Work through longer expressions carefully
  • Look for simplification opportunities
  • Combine multiple fraction skills

Attempt one or two Black questions.

Homework

Copy the assigned questions onto a separate sheet of paper and show all of your work.

Your goal is to demonstrate clear steps and accurate fraction work.
