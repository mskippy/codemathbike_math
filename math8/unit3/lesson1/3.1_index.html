Overview

In this lesson, we define rational numbers as numbers that can be written as fractions, decimals, and percents. We learn how these forms are connected and how to convert between them efficiently.

Learning Targets

  • Understand what a rational number is (in fraction, decimal, and percent form)
  • Convert between fractions, decimals, and percents
  • Compare and order rational numbers

Big Idea

A rational number can be written in different forms — fraction, decimal, and percent — but all forms represent the same value.

Choosing the most useful form depends on the situation.

Lesson Flow

Card Sort Activity

  • Sort rational number cards into equivalent groups
  • Defend your reasoning
  • Discuss repeating vs terminating decimals

Conversion Practice

  • Convert between fractions, decimals, and percents
  • Use efficient strategies
  • Check for accuracy

Ordering & Reasoning

  • Order rational numbers in different forms
  • Use benchmarks (½, ¼, ¾, etc.)
  • Explain your reasoning clearly

Green • Blue • Black Tasks

  • Green — Convert friendly numbers
  • Blue — Convert less familiar numbers
  • Black — Explain reasoning and patterns

Everyone begins with Green. Move forward when you feel ready for challenge.
