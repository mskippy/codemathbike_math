Overview

In this lesson, we focus on dividing positive fractions and mixed numbers.

We will strengthen procedural fluency using Keep, Change, Flip, simplify strategically, and extend to multi-step multiplication and division expressions.

Learning Targets

  • Find reciprocals correctly
  • Divide fractions using Keep, Change, Flip
  • Divide fractions and whole numbers
  • Divide mixed numbers (convert to improper fractions)
  • Evaluate multiplication and division expressions left to right

Warm Up

We begin by reviewing reciprocals and how division connects to multiplication.

A number multiplied by its reciprocal equals 1 (as long as it is not zero).

Big Idea

Dividing by a fraction is the same as multiplying by its reciprocal.

Keep the first fraction, change division to multiplication, flip the second fraction, then multiply and simplify.

Lesson Flow

🟢 Green — Core Mechanics

  • Divide fractions
  • Divide fractions and whole numbers
  • Multiply and simplify

🔵 Blue — Mixed Numbers & Strategy

  • Convert mixed numbers to improper fractions
  • Apply Keep, Change, Flip
  • Cross-simplify before multiplying

⚫ Black — Multiplication & Division Together

  • Evaluate expressions with multiple operations
  • Work left to right
  • Simplify strategically

Green • Blue • Black Practice

Your handout progresses from core division skills to mixed numbers and multi-step multiplication/division expressions.

  • Green — Divide fractions correctly

  • Black — Multi-step multiplication and division

Complete all Green and Blue questions and attempt as many Black questions as possible.
