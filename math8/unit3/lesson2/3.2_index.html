Overview

In this lesson, we focus on the mechanics of adding and subtracting positive fractions and mixed numbers.

We will practice finding common denominators, converting mixed numbers when needed, and simplifying answers properly.

Learning Targets

  • Add fractions with like denominators
  • Find common denominators to add and subtract unlike fractions
  • Add and subtract mixed numbers
  • Simplify answers fully

Big Idea

Fractions represent parts of the same whole. To add or subtract them, the parts must be the same size.

If the denominators are different, we must rewrite the fractions so the parts match before combining them.

Lesson Flow

1. Like Denominators

  • Add or subtract the numerators
  • Keep the denominator the same
  • Simplify if possible

2. Unlike Denominators

  • Find a common denominator
  • Rewrite both fractions
  • Add or subtract
  • Simplify

3. Mixed Numbers

  • Add/subtract whole numbers
  • Work with the fractional parts
  • Convert between mixed and improper fractions when helpful
  • Simplify fully

Green • Blue • Black Practice

  • Green — Like denominators and friendly numbers
  • Blue — Unlike denominators and mixed numbers
  • Black — Multi-step and more complex problems

Everyone begins with Green. Move forward when you are confident and accurate.

Homework

Complete your assigned Green, Blue, or Black questions on a separate sheet of paper.

Show all work clearly. Proper setup and simplification matter.
