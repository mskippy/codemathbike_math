Overview

In this lesson, we build the foundation for the unit by understanding percent as part-whole thinking. We connect percents to fractions, decimals, and visual models, and use familiar contexts to make sense of what a percent actually represents.

Learning Targets

  • Understand percent as “out of 100”
  • Represent percents using fractions, decimals, and visuals
  • Connect percent to familiar real-world contexts
  • Reason about benchmark percents like 50%, 25%, 10%, and 1%

Big Idea

Percent is another way of describing a part of a whole. It tells us how much out of 100.

Strong percent reasoning starts with understanding the meaning before using formulas.

Lesson Flow

Warm-Up & Notice / Wonder

  • Interpret visual models showing shaded portions
  • Discuss what percent means in everyday language
  • Connect to prior work with fractions and decimals

Building Meaning

  • Represent percents as fractions out of 100
  • Write equivalent decimals and percents
  • Use benchmarks like 50%, 25%, 75%, and 10%

Reasoning in Context

  • Interpret percents in contexts like test scores, discounts, and surveys
  • Decide whether an answer is reasonable
  • Explain thinking using words, numbers, and models

Green • Blue • Black Tasks

  • Green — Convert friendly percents, fractions, and decimals
  • Blue — Interpret percent in context and use benchmark reasoning
  • Black — Explain why equivalent forms represent the same amount

Start with Green, then move to Blue and Black when you are ready for more challenge.
