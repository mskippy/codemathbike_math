Overview

In this lesson, we learn how to find the percent of a number using mental math, benchmark percents, estimation, and calculation strategies. The focus is on understanding how the strategies work, not just following a rule.

Learning Targets

  • Find a percent of a number using benchmark strategies
  • Use 10%, 1%, 5%, 25%, and 50% to build other percents
  • Estimate to check whether an answer makes sense
  • Solve percent problems in practical contexts

Big Idea

Finding a percent of a number means finding a part of the whole. Efficient strategies often come from breaking the percent into easier benchmark amounts.

Flexible thinking is often more powerful than using a single formula every time.

Lesson Flow

Strategy Review

  • Review benchmark percents
  • Discuss how to find 10%, 1%, 5%, 25%, and 50%
  • Use mental math where possible

Guided Examples

  • Find percents of numbers in school and shopping contexts
  • Compare different strategies for the same problem
  • Decide which strategy is most efficient

Practice & Problem Solving

  • Solve Green, Blue, and Black questions
  • Check answers using estimation
  • Explain how the strategy matches the situation

Green • Blue • Black Tasks

  • Green — Find simple percents of numbers using friendly values
  • Blue — Solve percent problems with an extra step or context
  • Black — Tackle multi-step percent reasoning and explain your strategy

The goal is not just to get the answer, but to choose and explain an efficient method.
